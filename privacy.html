Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

Short version

Wee Landlord stores the information you enter — properties, tenancies, contacts, cases, photographs and documents — on your iPhone and, if iCloud is turned on, in your own private iCloud account. We don't run a server, we don't see your data, and we don't sell or share it with anyone.

What the app stores

Everything you type or photograph while using the app: property addresses, tenancy and tenant details you choose to record, contractor and contact details, case reports, inspection findings, photographs, and the documents the app generates (written summaries, case records, exports).

Where it's kept

On your device at all times. If iCloud is signed in and available, the app also keeps an encrypted copy in your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so your records sync between your own devices and are backed up. Nobody at Wee Landlord can read, access or recover this data — it is never sent to a server we operate, because we don't operate one.

What we don't do

We don't track you, show you ads, use analytics SDKs, or collect any data about you or your tenants for our own purposes. The app's App Store privacy label reflects this: no data is collected by the developer.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers the purchase itself.

Notifications and photos

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Photos you add are stored with the rest of your record, on-device and in your private iCloud, and are only included in PDFs or exports you choose to create and send yourself.

Your control over the data

You can edit or delete any property, case, contact or photo at any time. Deleting the app removes the on-device copy; turning off iCloud for the app or deleting your iCloud data removes the synced copy. Export Everything in Settings lets you take a full copy of your records with you at any time.

Children

Wee Landlord is a record-keeping tool for landlords and isn't directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will appear here with a new date at the top, and in the Privacy policy screen inside the app.
