Support

Help with Wee Landlord, the damp and mould compliance record for Scottish landlords.

Is my data backed up?

Yes, if iCloud is signed in and available on your device. Settings → iCloud backup shows when your records last synced. Your data stays in your own private iCloud account — Wee Landlord never sees it.

How do I get a copy of my records?

Settings → Export everything generates a PDF for each property and bundles them, along with photographs, into a single export you can save or share.

How are the legal deadlines calculated?

Settings → Working days shows which weekdays count and which bank holidays are excluded. The figures come from the Scottish Government's statutory guidance on the Investigation and Commencement of Repair (Scotland) Regulations 2026. Wee Landlord calculates the deadlines; you remain responsible for compliance.

I subscribed but the app still shows the free plan

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. This re-checks your purchase history with the App Store. If it still doesn't update, make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or tap Manage plan inside Wee Landlord, which opens the same screen.

Does anyone else see my tenants' information?

No. Wee Landlord doesn't run a server and has no access to your records. Everything stays on your device and in your own private iCloud. See the privacy policy for details.
